Why it matters

Many store networks are still planned in spreadsheets

Teams with a few warehouses and many stores decide every day or week what to buy and where to send it. The cost shows up twice: lost sales where stock ran out, and cash tied up where it sits unsold.

Rules go stale

Min/max levels set once a year ignore seasonality, promotions and the supplier who just slipped two weeks.

Stockouts hide demand

An empty shelf records zero sales. The next forecast drops, and the stockout repeats.

Locations planned alone

Warehouses and stores compete for the same stock with no view of the whole network.

Suites mean a re-platform

Enterprise planning suites take months of integration work and are built and priced for the largest companies.

Data in, without a project

Drop the CSV or Excel exports you already have, or let your systems copy them to Amazon S3 or Azure, where MetaFlow checks for new files every hour. Each file is recognised from its columns and every row checked; a file that looks different from last time waits with one plain sentence. Confirm a reading once and the plan runs by itself from then on.

A forecast for every store

Statistical methods and a machine-learning model compete on each item's own history. Weeks with a stockout or a promotion are kept out of the baseline, and MetaFlow reads stockouts from your stock files when your sales export doesn't show them. A new item can borrow a similar item's history. Bring your own forecast and MetaFlow uses it where it covers.

One plan for the whole network

Supplier purchases and warehouse-to-store transfers are planned together, within case packs, minimums, storage, routes, order days, receiving days and budgets. A supplier order below its minimum value is filled from next week's need instead of being dropped, and the line says so. When warehouse stock runs short, it is shared across stores by risk and priority.

Your planners stay in charge

Every line explains itself in plain words: position, target, the pack and the one limit that shaped it. Planners keep, change or skip it with a reason, try a what-if, and download order files that import into their own system as they are. Ask MetaFlow answers their questions in plain words with the plan's own numbers, and Results compares forecasts and plans with what actually sold.

What a planner actually sees

Start with what needs attention

The plan opens on the lines: five numbers for the week, what needs attention (items that run out before the next possible delivery, lines cut by a limit, what changed since your last review) and then every purchase and transfer, most urgent first or grouped by supplier.

  • Urgency, order date, arrival and days of cover on every line
  • Keep, change or skip in one click; a change or skip takes a reason
  • Re-plans by itself when fresh stock or open orders arrive, keeps your decisions, and flags only the lines that changed

Every line answers the planner's questions

Planners don't trust numbers they can't check, so every line shows its arithmetic in one sentence.

  • Why this quantity: position, target (demand over the lead time and review period plus safety stock), rounded to whole packs
  • Order by, arrives, usable and runs out, with the one limit that shaped the line: a pack, a supplier minimum, a budget
  • Every input behind one fold, and what changed since your last review

Test a change before you commit

Try higher or lower demand, a later supplier, a budget cap or a different service target, and see the result beside the plan.

  • Order value, projected stockouts, ending stock and lines changed
  • Up to three what-ifs per plan; none of them changes the real plan, its decisions or its order files

Security

Safe to put next to your system of record

MetaFlow reads files and recommends. Your ERP stays in charge of every transaction.

Read-only by design

Read-only storage credentials, encrypted at rest. MetaFlow never changes your files or writes back. Its operational logs hold ids, counts and timings, never your items, prices or sales.

Multi-tenant from day one

Every query is scoped to your workspace, with automated tests on isolation.

Roles and audit

Owners, admins, planners and viewers, invitations limited to your company's email domains, and a full audit log. MetaFlow support can look only when an admin allows it: read-only, for 48 hours, and recorded.

People decide

No automatic orders, reservations or approvals. Every decision is recorded with its reason.
