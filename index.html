Platform modules

Inventory operations and planning in one place

MetaFlow combines operational inventory control with demand intelligence, replenishment optimization, risk analysis, and AI-assisted workflows.

Inventory Control Hub

Creates a trusted view of products, suppliers, locations, stock, orders, and movements.

  • Product and SKU catalog
  • Supplier and manufacturer records
  • Purchase and sales order context
  • Barcode, batch, serial, and label support

POS Data Layer

Connects sales activity to planning signals so demand is based on real customer movement.

  • Daily sales and returns
  • Store-level movement
  • Promotions and seasonal patterns
  • Stockout and fast-mover signals

Demand Forecasting Engine

Predicts future demand using statistical and machine learning forecasting methods.

  • SKU, store, warehouse, and region forecasts
  • Short-term and long-term views
  • Confidence ranges
  • Demand anomaly detection

Replenishment Optimizer

Recommends what to order, how much to order, when to order, and where to place inventory.

  • Multi-echelon inventory planning
  • Supplier-to-warehouse planning
  • Warehouse-to-store replenishment
  • Service-level and cost trade-offs

Simulation Studio

Tests operational risk before teams commit inventory, capital, or supplier actions.

  • Demand spike scenarios
  • Supplier delay scenarios
  • Lead-time and safety stock changes
  • Store, warehouse, and transfer decisions

Agentic AI Layer

Uses AI agents to explain changes, monitor exceptions, summarize risks, and guide actions.

  • Inventory risk detection
  • Forecast explanation
  • Replenishment recommendations
  • Planning summaries and workflow support
