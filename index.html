The problem

Most networks are still planned one location at a time.

Rules go stale.

Min and max levels set once a year ignore seasons, promotions and the supplier who just slipped two weeks.

Stockouts hide demand.

An empty shelf records zero sales. The next forecast drops, and the stockout repeats.

Every location plans alone.

Warehouses and stores compete for the same stock, and nobody sees the whole network.

The idea

The least total stock that meets your service targets, on the network you already have.

MetaFlow IQ treats your supply chain as one network of places and the links between them, and plans all of it together. That is multi-echelon inventory optimization (MEIO), delivered as a service.

  • Mid-size and large companies
  • Tens of thousands of SKUs
  • Many distribution centers, warehouses and stores
  • Retail and consumer goods
